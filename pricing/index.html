Questions

Before you start

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with 14 days free. You choose a plan when you create your account; nothing is charged until the trial ends, and you can change plan at any time.

Monthly or yearly?

Pay monthly, or pay once a year and save 5%. Prices are shown per month so the plans are easy to compare, and you can switch in Settings at any time.

Why do I request access?

We open Finvry to new companies in small groups so each one is set up properly. Request access and we email you an invitation, usually within the week. A partner or community code from your VC, accelerator or community skips the queue.

Can I pay in naira?

Yes. Companies in Nigeria are billed in naira by card, bank transfer or your Finvry wallet. Everywhere else is billed in US dollars, and your local card works.

Do company services cost extra?

Yes, they are priced per service and the same on every plan. They are optional: keep your own accountant or registered agent if you have one.
