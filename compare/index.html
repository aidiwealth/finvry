Everything investors ask for

Cap table, updates, data room, deck, forecast, statements and the annual report share the same numbers, for one company or a group with subsidiaries in several currencies, so what you send is always consistent.

AI that does the paperwork

Grants, share purchases and board consents drafted for you, imports read from any file, report details filled from your account or online, and an analyst that tells you what to do next.
