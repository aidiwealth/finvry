What does Finvry do?
Finvry keeps the investor side of your company in one place: metrics, fundraising, compliance and investor updates, with AI that drafts the work.
Does Finvry hold or move money?
No. Finvry is software. It reads balances to show burn and runway, and never moves funds.
Which tools does it connect to?
QuickBooks, Google Sheets, Excel, PDF statements and bank accounts, plus the Finvry Data API.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan covers your dashboard, annual report, investor page and data room. Paid plans start with 14 days free.
Where is Finvry available?
The software works anywhere. Company services are offered in the United States and parts of Africa.
